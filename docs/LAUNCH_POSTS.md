## English: niche job-board and newsletter builders

I built HiringSignal for a specific workflow: turning selected public employer
job feeds into a shortlist for an AI/Data newsletter or niche job board.

You can choose a curated employer group and role preset, then download the
saved matching jobs as CSV/JSON plus a Markdown newsletter draft. It reads
public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby feeds and keeps the source application links.

For the next run, pass the saved job-key array back in to skip known results.
That is a seen-list: it does not prove a job was newly posted or detect edits
and closures. The watchlist is small, not a global jobs search, and a remote
label does not establish worldwide eligibility.

The configured Actor price is $0.0015 per saved job ($1.50 per 1,000); see the
Pricing tab and displayed platform charges. A six-job starter input limits the
first trial to at most six saved rows. There are no guaranteed matching counts.

Bạn chọn nhóm công ty và loại việc có sẵn, rồi tải CSV/JSON hoặc bản nháp

## Evidence to collect from a real trial

- Does the person actually maintain a newsletter, job board or employer watchlist?
- Can they select useful boards and filters without help?
- Which output do they use, and what manual editing remains?
- Do they return for a second run with their saved keys?
- Did the platform record a paying customer and actual revenue? Record those
  separately from free trials, owner tests and saved-job counts.

Ask for workflow feedback in an appropriate opted-in setting. These drafts do
not authorize automated messages, unsolicited bulk outreach or posting on the
user's behalf.
